1. Understanding Solar Energy

Published on 10 November 2026 by SkyGrid Energy Solutions.

Solar energy is electricity or heat obtained from the sun. Photovoltaic panels convert sunlight directly into electrical energy through the photovoltaic effect.

A typical solar power system contains solar panels, an inverter, electrical protection devices and, where required, battery storage.

How Does a Solar Power System Work?

  1. Solar panels receive sunlight.
  2. The photovoltaic cells produce direct current (DC).
  3. The inverter converts DC electricity into alternating current (AC).
  4. AC electricity supplies compatible household or business loads.
  5. Excess energy may charge batteries where storage is installed.

Solar energy can help reduce dependence on conventional electricity sources when the system is properly designed and installed.

2. Understanding Solar Battery Storage

Published on 10 November 2026 by SkyGrid Energy Solutions.

Battery energy storage systems store electrical energy for use when solar production is insufficient or unavailable.

Battery storage is particularly useful at night, during cloudy periods and when grid electricity is unavailable.

Common Battery Technologies

  • Lithium-ion batteries: Commonly used in modern solar energy storage systems.
  • Lead-acid batteries: An established battery technology used in various backup power applications.

Important Factors When Selecting a Battery

  • Battery capacity measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh).
  • Maximum charging and discharging power.
  • Permitted depth of discharge.
  • Battery management system compatibility.
  • Expected operating temperature and service life.

Selecting the right battery requires consideration of the customer's energy consumption, backup requirements and inverter specifications.

Category: Energy Storage Technology

3. Why Solar System Maintenance Matters

Published on 10 November 2026 by SkyGrid Energy Solutions.

Regular solar system maintenance helps identify problems before they develop into major equipment failures. It also supports safe and reliable operation.

Basic Solar Maintenance Practices

  1. Inspect solar panels for visible damage and dirt.
  2. Check cables, connectors and electrical enclosures.
  3. Monitor inverter performance and system alarms.
  4. Inspect battery condition and operating temperature.
  5. Review system performance and energy production records.

Electrical inspections and repairs should be performed by appropriately qualified personnel using suitable safety procedures.
